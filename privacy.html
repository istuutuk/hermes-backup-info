Hermes Backup — Privacy Policy

This policy describes the personal Hermes Backup utility operated by the owner of GitHub account istuutuk.

Data and purpose

The utility accesses the owner's approved local Hermes files for recovery backups and Google Drive files created or explicitly selected for this utility. Archives may contain configuration, skills, memory and conversation history; the exact contents, encryption and retention are chosen by the owner before recurring uploads are enabled.

Storage and credentials

OAuth credentials are kept in restricted files on the owner's server and are used only to authenticate requests to Google Drive. They are not published in this website or its public repository. Approved backup archives are stored on the owner's server and Google Drive, not on GitHub Pages.

Sharing and AI use

This backup utility does not sell data, share archives with advertising providers, or send archive contents to AI services for training, inference or analysis. It is separate from any AI services the owner uses in Hermes itself. Google processes uploaded files under Google's applicable terms and privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Recurring backups and automated deletion are currently disabled. The owner must approve a retention policy before these are enabled. The owner can remove backups directly from their server or Google Drive and revoke this utility's access through Google Account security settings. Revocation does not automatically delete existing archives.

Website hosting

This informational website has no custom analytics, forms, advertising or tracking scripts. GitHub Pages hosts the pages and may process visitor connection information under GitHub's privacy policy. Do not submit credentials, archives or private conversations in public GitHub issues.

Google API policy

The utility's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Contact

Contact the operator via the information repository. This public channel should be used only for non-sensitive questions.
